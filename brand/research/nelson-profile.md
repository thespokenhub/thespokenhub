## 6. Voice analysis (from his LinkedIn posts, Substack, agency blog, MinCave article, personal blog)

- **Lowercase-first on LinkedIn**: personal posts often start lowercase and keep "i" lowercase, with occasional capitals for emphasis ("BEIGE-ification", "DON'T"). The longer blog and Substack writing uses standard caps.
- **Trailing ellipses and stretched words**: "and......", "remotely…..", "butttt", "swearrrrrrrr", "THANK YEWWW", "ahhhkkk h*ll yeah", "ermmmm......".
- **Parenthetical asides and self-deprecation**: "(and yes, i'm using an overgeneralization because I'm tired)", "(cough cough… I know it shouldn't)", "(don't!)", "(Okay fine, we're definitely counting.)", "(yes, we said digging it)".
- **Short, one-line paragraphs**, with a lesson in the last line. The pattern is: personal anecdote, then the realization, then a low-key practical takeaway ("the email takes ten minutes. write it, rest, deal with the rest when you're human again.").
- **Honest, anti-hype, "no-BS" stance**: "I'm no-nonsense and honest. I tell it like it is." He uses "Harsh, I know." and "And that, my friend, is not us." He calls out "polished thought leadership BS". He builds in public with real numbers ($80, 30 emails, 13 subscribers).
- **Recurring themes**: beige or sameness in B2B content; quality over volume ("Better is better"); writing for buyers, not audiences; research obsession ("I spend too much time researching (and I feel it's still not enough)"); customer interviews and customer language; strategy before production; patience and the content-to-pipeline lag; AI as amplifier, not replacement; the freelancer/agency life (rates, payments, illness, client fear).
- **Signature phrases**: "Walk with me." "Thing is,…" "Heck,…" "smacking" (as in "a smacking meal"); "content so good you don't realize it's marketing"; "spicy" ideas; "kick-ass"/"kickass"; "the icky bits"; "2 AM group chat"; "scrappy"; "Wild, I know, but true."; "Your call."; "luv".
- **Cultural references**: Bill Burr, Claude Hopkins / Schlitz, First Round Review, Ryan Law ("shtick"), Brendan Hufford ("Checkbox Marketing"), Gong, Drift, Groove, TikTok vs YouTube.
- **Self-image**: the agency copy says "I say overthinking is overrated… I'm the one skipping the overthinking and getting things done." The newsletter jokes the opposite: "If it feels meticulous and overthought, blame Nelson."
- Light Ghanaian/global-remote framing ("most of my clients are based in the US. I am not.").
- Avoid in ghostwriting (his own complaints): generic hooks, carousels, "contrarian" takes nobody disagrees with, "synergy" language, "I hope this email finds you well", guru "secret sauce" talk, and anything that reads like "written by robots for robots".
