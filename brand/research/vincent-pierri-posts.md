I looked at everything from $20 to $1,000 a month memberships. I would NOT have guessed the most expensive one I found was a community for people who waterproof basements. 

!!!

Not only was it led by a non-famous person...
There wasn’t even a name attached to it.

Just a website.
Who would’ve thought.

Here were the 3 biggest things I learned from the research:

1️⃣ The more expensive the community, the LESS content matters.

At the low end, people are paying for:
→ Tools
→ Content
→ Resources

At the high end, they’re paying for:
→ Access
→ Feedback
→ Relationships

2️⃣ A big audience matters less as your price goes up.

See above.

Cheap communities need reach.

Premium ones can work with a much smaller audience if you have:
→ Niche authority
→ Strong relationships
→ The right buyers
→ A good paid ads strategy

3️⃣ Your job changes depending on what you charge.

Low price:
→ More content
→ More admin
→ More members

High price:
→ More coaching
→ More people
→ More personal attention
