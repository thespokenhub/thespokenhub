# Nelson's LinkedIn Playbook

The single source of truth for every post we write. Read this before drafting anything.

Built from: Nelson's 22 reference posts (`reference-examples.md`), real scraped posts from Brinda Gulati (13), Joe Meier (9) and Vincent Pierri (10 + graphics), and Nelson's own posts, portfolio, agency site and newsletter (`research/`).

---

## 0. Direction (set by Nelson, 6 Oct 2026)

- **This is a hangout, not a storefront.** Nelson's LinkedIn is a safe place and an outlet: connect with people, share opinions, laugh, make puns. No agency pitch, no lead magnet (for now).
- **Less me, more them.** Posts are about the reader's world (their drafts, their blog, their stakeholders, their Tuesday), not Nelson's credentials. And the voice leans on Brinda, Joe and Vincent more than on Nelson's old posts.
- **Never name clients.** No client names, no client numbers. Talk about "a founder I worked with" or keep it general.
- **The Other Guys** can come up naturally ("me and Tim") but is never the point of a post.
- **Posts, not articles.**
- **Brand colour: cyan.**

## 1. The diagnosis: why LinkedIn hasn't worked yet

| What's happening now | What it costs |
|---|---|
| 4 personal posts in 2026, months apart | No momentum. The algorithm and the audience both forget you. |
| Posts open with "own musings here....... ignore" | They tell the reader to skip them. |
| No opinions anyone could argue with, no jokes anyone would share | 0 comments on every personal post. |
| Best post (16 likes) is an honest money confession | People respond to honest, specific, insider stuff. Do more of that, and add humor. |

**The fix:** post 4–5x a week. Mix opinions about content and startup marketing, craft tips people can steal, and jokes about the job. Every post should make a founder think "he gets it" and a content person think "I'm sending this to my team chat."

## 2. Positioning (the vibe, not a pitch)

> The content guy who says what everyone in the team chat is thinking, then explains why it matters.

- **Enemy:** beige content. Same hooks, same "contrarian" takes, articles shipped to fill a calendar, content "written by robots for robots."
- **Belief:** the juice is in the thinking, not the writing.
- **Proof:** not client logos. The proof is that every post is useful, specific, and funny enough to share.

## 3. Audience → what each post must give them

| Who | What they want from the feed | Post types that serve them |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders (pre-A to B)** | "Will content actually make me money? When? What do I do first?" | Pipeline/ROI takes, "don't publish yet", positioning, first-90-days, hiring an agency vs freelancer vs in-house |
| **CMOs / Heads of Marketing** | Ammo for the board, frameworks, ways to make the team better | Frameworks, metrics that matter, case breakdowns, briefs, distribution |
| **Growth / demand gen** | Content that converts, not just ranks | Conversion teardowns, BOFU content, repurposing, AI-search visibility |
| **Amplifiers (content managers, writers, brand marketers, operators)** | Craft tips, validation, things to share with their boss | Writing craft, editing, interview tactics, freelancer realities, memes |

Rule of thumb: **amplifiers spread you, buyers hire you.** Craft posts get reach. Pipeline posts get DMs. Do both every week.

## 4. Voice: the blend

Ingredients, in order of weight:

1. **Brinda Gulati (~35%)** is the personality. Smart and camp, with literary and pop-culture references used flippantly, a parenthetical punchline, CAPS for shouting, and 🫡 as a sign-off.
2. **Nelson's reference posts (~30%)** are the skeleton for teaching posts: direct, short lines, always explaining the WHY.
3. **Joe Meier (~25%)** sets the comedy bar. Deadpan one-liners and LinkedIn or agency-life satire, never explaining the joke and never ending with a CTA.
4. **Nelson's own natural voice (~10%)** is a pinch: "Thing is,", "Harsh, I know.", stretched words ("butttt", "OKKKKKK"), "(don't!)", the Ghana-with-US-clients reality.

**Puns are welcome.** One per post at most, and never in the hook of a serious teaching post.

### Voice rules

- **Explain the WHY.** Every claim gets a reason or an example (reference copy 3 and copy 10). No naked statements.
- **Specific over clever.** Name the tool, the meeting, the doc name, the exact phrase a stakeholder used. "final_FINAL_v3.docx" beats "messy files."
- **One idea per post.** If there are two, it's two posts.
- **Opinions with a spine.** Take a side someone could actually disagree with. No "contrarian takes that nobody could disagree with" (Nelson's own complaint).
- **Promise less, deliver more.** No "this will change your life" (reference copy 16).
- **Personality, not oversharing** (reference copy 7). A parenthetical aside, a confession, a joke. Not a diary.
- **Talk to people who already tried and failed** (reference copy 21). Founders who already burned money on content, and writers who already took the hooks course.
- **Make it about them.** Count the "I"s and "you"s. Unless it's a personal story post, "you" should win.

### Formatting rules

- **Capitalise properly.** Retire the all-lowercase habit for main posts. It reads as "ignore me." Lowercase is fine for meme one-liners only (Joe/Brinda style).
- **Short lines.** 1–2 sentences per paragraph and a blank line between them.
- **Length:** 3 modes (see §5). Most teaching posts run 80–220 words; never over ~300.
- **Lists:** use `-` dashes or `1.` numbers (from the reference posts). `•` is fine occasionally. Use `→` only for flows.
- **CAPS for one or two words of emphasis** per post: WHY, BEFORE, BIG mistake.
- **Unicode bold (𝗹𝗶𝗸𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝘀):** max one line per post (the hook or the thesis), and only in Brinda-style posts. Screen readers struggle with it, so use it sparingly.
- **Em dashes:** Brinda uses them freely. Cap at 1–2 per post so it doesn't read as AI.
- **Emojis:** 0–3 per post, used as punctuation, never as bullets. House set: 🫡 ✂️ 🧠 🧲 👋 👇 💅🏾 🥲 😅. No 🚀🔥💯.
- **No hashtags.** None of the three creators use them.
- **No links in the post body.** Put them in the first comment ("link in comments 👇") or use the Featured section.
- **Endings:** a punchline, a one-line takeaway, a short question ("What'd I miss?", "You feel me?"), or nothing at all. Never "Agree? 👇 Like and repost ♻️."

### Banned words and moves (AI tells + beige)

delve, unlock, unleash, game-changer, leverage (as a verb), elevate, supercharge, in today's fast-paced world, it's not X, it's Y (as a lazy crutch; at most once a week), "Here's the thing:" as an opener, "Let that sink in", "I hope this helps", "synergy", "secret sauce" (unless mocking it), fake dialogue like "My client said: 'Nelson, you changed my life'", invented stats, triple-adjective stacks.

## 5. Three post modes

### Mode A: The Teacher (≈30% of posts)
Long-ish (100–220 words), from reference copy 3, 5, 6, 11, 12, 16, 21 and 22.
Shape: **sharp claim → why it matters → example/breakdown → takeaway line.** Often comes with a framework graphic.

### Mode B: The Hot Take / Aphorism (≈35%)
Short (15–80 words), from the lead example, reference copy 1, 7, 10, 13, 17, 18 and 20, and Brinda's structured posts.
Shape: **2–6 punchy lines, one opinion, a twist or parenthetical punchline at the end.**

### Mode C: The One-Liner / Meme (≈35%)
1–2 lines, Joe and Brinda style. Deadpan, relatable, about content/startup/LinkedIn life. Often paired with a Vincent-style meme graphic. These are the reach engines: Joe's top two got 3.8K and 4.2K reactions, Brinda's top got 741, Vincent's top memes got 211–340.

Content pillars:

| Pillar | Share | Example angles |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Content that actually works (POV) | 30% | Traffic ≠ pipeline; write for buyers; fewer, better pieces; distribution |
| 2. The thinking behind the writing (craft) | 30% | Customer interviews, briefs, editing, the WHY, specificity, killing beige |
| 3. The job, but funny | 30% | Stakeholder reviews, final_FINAL_v3, LinkedIn itself, AI slop, deadlines |
| 4. The human bit | 10% | Remote from Ghana, raising rates, getting sick mid-project, freelancer feelings |

## 6. Hook library (first 1–2 lines)

Each hook comes from a reference post or creator. Swap in the subject.

- **Dear [audience],** "Dear founder, there's no secret sauce." (lead example)
- **Huge mistake + named mistake:** "Huge mistake when [doing X]: [the mistake]." (copy 3)
- **Number + list promise:** "The best [role] avoid these [N] mistakes:" (copy 5)
- **"Nobody / Most people" claim:** "Nobody builds [result] on [tactic] alone." (copy 6)
- **"I'm over…":** "I'm over the term '[buzzword].'" (copy 9)
- **Fake-ridiculous number:** "[Field] has 1,929,746 rules." (copy 18)
- **Scam call-out:** "'[Popular tactic]' is the biggest scam in [field]." (copy 17)
- **Quoted question:** "'[Question your audience asks]'" (copy 12)
- **"Notice how…":** "Notice how when you [common experience]… It never [result]?" (copy 16)
- **Two things:** "Two things I'll always lie about:" (copy 14)
- **Inverted target:** "Your [X] should always address [unexpected group]. Not [expected group]." (copy 21)
- **Timely trigger:** "Q4 starts [day]. If you're [pain], read this first." (Brinda)
- **Pulled quote from a deliverable:** "'[A line from a client piece]'" (Brinda)
- **Fake-sincere update:** "Just another incredible day without [X]." (Joe)
- **Conspiracy frame:** "[Org] doesn't want you to know that [absurd claim]." (Joe)
- **In a world full of:** "In a world full of [AI slop] — be [unexpected thing]." (Joe)
- **Confession:** "The first time I [did scary thing], I assumed [wrong thing]." (Nelson's best post)

## 7. Graphics spec (Vincent Pierri's system, adapted)

Reference files are in `research/vincent-graphics/`.

**What we copy:** the system. That means white canvas, a big title with key words in "pills", a hand-lettered accent font, a pastel category palette, dense frameworks or dead-simple memes, and no logos.

**What we change:** the signature colour. Lavender pills are Vincent's brand recognition. Nelson's are **cyan**. We also drop lavender from the palette entirely so the two never get confused.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1080×1350 (4:5) default; 1080×1080 for memes |
| Background | #FFFFFF / #FCFCFD. Never dark. |
| Title | "How to [PILL] a [PILL]" pattern. Plain words in Manrope Light/Regular at 72–90px, black, tight tracking. |
| Pills | Cyan #7FDDEB fill, rounded rect (radius 14px), 2–3px border in deep teal #0E4F5C, soft grey drop shadow, ALL-CAPS hand-lettered text (Gochi Hand / Patrick Hand SC) in #0B1A1F |
| Subtitle | One line under the title, Manrope Bold 22–26px, saying who it's for or what you get ("For founders who are tired of blog posts nobody reads.") |
| Body | Manrope Regular 16–22px for labels, Bold/ExtraBold for headers. No sentences, only 2–5 word phrases. |
| Palette | Hero **cyan #7FDDEB** (tint #D6F5FA), then coral #F5A9A0 (tint #FCE1DD), butter #F5E08C (tint #FBF3D2), periwinkle #A5C5EF (tint #D9E7F8), mint #A7EDB1 (tint #DAF7DE). Text #0B1A1F. |
| Lines/arrows | 2px deep teal #0E4F5C, right-angle elbows, small arrowheads |
| Graphic types | (1) numbered system bands 1–4, (2) comparison/cheat-sheet table, (3) before→after pills, (4) joke line chart ("High/Low", "Start → One Year Later"), (5) Venn of 3 pastel circles, (6) UI parody (toggle switches, progress bars), (7) "POV:" pill + one line |
| Branding | None on the graphic. Optional tiny "Nelson Ansah" credit in the bottom corner at 12px grey. |
| Motion (optional) | Subtle GIF: arrows draw on, rows fade in, 3–8s loop, ending on the full static frame |

**How text and graphic split:**
- **Framework posts:** the post tells the story and the graphic is the shareable cheat sheet ("I put the whole thing in the graphic below.").
- **Meme posts:** a 2–6 word caption, and the graphic does the work.
- **Hot takes:** the graphic restates the hook in pills.

## 10. Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Does the hook say who it's for and why they should care?
- [ ] Is every claim followed by a WHY or an example?
- [ ] Is there at least one specific (number, client type, tool, timeframe)?
- [ ] Could someone reasonably disagree? (If not, sharpen it.)
- [ ] Is there one moment of personality (aside, confession, joke)?
- [ ] No banned words, hashtags or links in the body?
- [ ] No more than 2 em dashes or 3 emojis?
- [ ] No client names, no client numbers, no invented stats?
- [ ] Is it more about "you" than "me"?
- [ ] Would a founder screenshot it to their team, or a content person drop it in the team chat?
